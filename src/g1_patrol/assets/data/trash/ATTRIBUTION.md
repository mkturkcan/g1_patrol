# Litter and trash-bag models

The `.usda` items are generated by `scripts/assets/make_nyc_trash.py` (no third-party content). The `.usdc`
items are imported by `scripts/assets/import_litter_assets.py` from the models below, which were rescaled,
re-centered, reduced to the listed parts, converted to USD and given a convex collision hull and a mass.
